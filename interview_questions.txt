1. Decision tree: recursively splits data into branches and predicts at leaf nodes.
2. Entropy: impurity/uncertainty; information gain: reduction in impurity after a split.
3. Random forest: combines many trees to improve generalization and reduce variance.
4. Overfitting: great training performance but poorer unseen-data performance; control depth, minimum samples, pruning, or use ensembles.
5. Bagging: trains models on bootstrap samples and combines predictions.
6. Visualization: sklearn plot_tree() or Graphviz.
7. Feature importance: relative contribution to model splits; it does not prove causation.
8. Random forest: accurate and robust, but less interpretable and more computationally expensive than one tree.
